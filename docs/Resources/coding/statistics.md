Course: [Research Methods and Statistics in Psychology Specialization](https://www.coursera.org/specializations/sage-research-methods-and-statistics-in-psychology) by SAGE on coursera
- Design and evaluate psychological research using appropriate qualitative and quantitative methodologies.
- Apply statistical techniques to analyze, interpret, and evaluate psychological research findings.
- Apply ethical principles and critically assess research designs, methods, and evidence in psychology.

Course: [Statistics in Psychological Research](https://www.coursera.org/learn/statistics-in-psychological-research) on coursera
- Explain ways to categorize variables and describe data.
- Describe how graphs are used to visualize data.
- Describe the logic of inferential statistics and null hypothesis significance testing.
- Select the appropriate inferential test based on criteria.
- Compare and contrast the use of statistical significance, effect size, and confidence intervals.
- Explain the importance of statistical power.  
- Describe how alternative procedures address the major objections to null hypothesis significance testing.

Book: [An R companion to Statistical Thinking for the 21st Century](https://statsthinking21.github.io/statsthinking21-R-site/) by Russell Poldrack
- focuses on R statistical programming language and data analysis

Book: [Handbook of Functional MRI Data Analysis](https://rutgers.app.box.com/s/bccv8sui4xnbbecp92e48j9pmxfqb5g5) by Russell Poldrack (2011)
- provides a comprehensive and practical introduction to the methods used for fMRI data analysis. 
- Using minimal jargon, this book explains the concepts behind processing fMRI data, focusing on the techniques that are most commonly used in the field. 
- This book provides background about the methods employed by common data analysis packages including FSL, SPM, and AFNI. 
- Some of the newest cutting-edge techniques, including pattern classification analysis, connectivity modeling, and resting state network analysis, are also discussed.

Book: [Statistical Analysis of fMRI Data.pdf](https://rutgers.app.box.com/s/njte4uq9dqv47fgv5dqjomjan6i6e3r3) by F. Gregory Ashby (2011)
- The goal of this book is for readers to understand the most important current methods for analyzing fMRI data.
- The goal for each method is for readers to understand the basic underlying logic, the assumptions, the strengths and weaknesses, and when the method is or is not appropriate to apply
- The book assumes no prior background in fMRI. The only special background needed is basic statistical inference
